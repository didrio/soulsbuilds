Plan, share and follow Elden Ring builds

A free build planner: put a build together with live stats, follow a step-by-step route to every item in it, and share it with one link. No account needed to start.

Plan a build

Pick weapons, armor, talismans and spells. Stats, HP, equip load and poise update as you choose.

Follow it in your run

The Guide shows where every item is, in the order you’ll reach it. Check them off as you go.

Share and discover

One link shows the whole build. Browse what other players run, and like the ones you’d try.
