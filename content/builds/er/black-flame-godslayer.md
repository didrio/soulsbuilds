A late-game Faith caster built on black flame from the Godslayer's Seal, with Light of Miquella and Scarlet Aeonia for the end of the DLC.

## How it plays

Black Flame is your main attack. It sets the target on fire, and the burn eats a share of the enemy's maximum health, so it hits bosses hardest. Throw it at enemies while they close in, keep your distance, and recast whenever the burn runs out. Before a boss, cast Golden Vow and Black Flame's Protection; the two stack. When the boss gives you time, Black Blade takes a big chunk of its health. In the second phase, Scarlet Aeonia spreads scarlet rot for a damage-over-time finish. Light of Miquella, from the end of the DLC, clears groups.

The Black Knife's Blade of Death is a second percentage-based attack, and the Sword of Darkness is for when something gets too close.

## Why these pieces

- **Godslayer's Seal** boosts black flame incantations and keeps scaling at high Faith.
- **Flock's Canvas Talisman** boosts every incantation, **Radagon Icon** speeds up casting, **Cerulean Amber Medallion +3** gives a bigger FP pool, and **Dragoncrest Greatshield** cuts physical damage.
- **Godskin Apostle set**: our pick, since the source names no armor. It's light enough for a quick roll and fits the black flame theme.
- Swap Erdtree Heal in for Scarlet Aeonia when you need a heal.

## Stats

72 Faith for Light of Miquella and the incantations' damage. 43 Mind for a deep FP pool, 58 Vigor, and Dexterity 33 for casting speed and the swords.
