The Tarnished Pack's Heavy Knight, early game: a Heavy Hefty Scimitar with Cragblade, the Lordsworn's Shield and guard counters. For Limgrave and Liurnia.

## How it plays

This is a blocking build. Hold the shield up through an enemy's combo, take the last hit on the shield, and answer with a guard counter (heavy attack right after a block). Two or three counters break most enemies' stance; then take the critical hit. Cragblade adds damage and stance damage to the scimitar for a while, so cast it before a fight starts.

Patch 1.17 made this route stronger: the Lordsworn's Shield now blocks better and weighs less.

## Why these pieces

- **Hefty Scimitar**: the class's own weapon, a curved greatsword with low requirements. **Heavy affinity** moves its scaling onto Strength.
- **Cragblade** comes from a Teardrop Scarab in Dragonbarrow, a quick detour through Caelid. The Guide tab has the route to every piece.
- **Lordsworn's Shield** drops from Godrick Soldiers who carry shields.
- **Curved Sword Talisman** boosts guard counters, the build's main damage. **Arsenal Charm** lets you carry more weight.
- **Greenspill Crystal Tear** gives more stamina for blocking; the **Strength-knot Crystal Tear** gives +10 Strength for three minutes.
- **The full Steel set** is heavy. At this level you have a heavy roll, which a shield build can live with. If you'd rather roll faster, swap the greaves for something lighter.

## Stats

Every level goes into Vigor, Endurance and Strength: 30, 25 and 30. Mind, Dexterity and the magic stats stay at the class's base. This build keeps going into the endgame as the Heavy Knight Greatshield Tank.
