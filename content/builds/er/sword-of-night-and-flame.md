Sword of Night and Flame with Intelligence first and enough Faith for Golden Vow and death sorceries: a ranged-blast spellblade. Patch 1.17.

## How it plays

The sword's skill, Night-and-Flame Stance, fires a magic beam or a fire sweep, depending on which attack you press after it. Either one takes a large chunk of a boss's health. Use it when a boss leaves an opening, then swing the sword normally while your FP recovers.

The death sorceries from the scepter fill the gaps. Rancorcall sends homing spirits, and Ancient Death Rancor sends a large volley of them. Cast Golden Vow from the seal before boss fights, and stand in Terra Magica while you cast for bigger hits.

## Why these pieces

- **Carian Knight's Shield** blocks well against magic attacks while you hold the sword in one hand.
- **Magic Scorpion Charm** and **Shard of Alexander** boost the skill's magic and the skill itself. **Assassin's Cerulean Dagger** restores FP after critical hits, and **Great-Jar's Arsenal** carries the Night's Cavalry and Lionel's armor at a medium roll.
- **Frenzied Flame Seal** is a light seal for Golden Vow.

## Stats

66 Intelligence for the sword and the sorceries. 25 Faith covers Golden Vow (25) and Ancient Death Rancor (24). 50 Vigor, 25 Mind for skill and spell use, 30 Endurance for the armor.
