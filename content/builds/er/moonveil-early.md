Moonveil in the early game: a katana that fires a magic wave, backed by a few cheap sorceries. Built from the Prisoner for Liurnia and beyond.

## How it plays

Moonveil is a katana whose skill, Transient Moonlight, draws the blade and sends a wave of magic forward. It reaches farther than it looks and hits hard for its FP cost. Most fights go like this: an opening Transient Moonlight, a few quick slashes, step back, and draw again when the enemy recovers.

The staff covers what the katana can't. Glintstone Pebble is a cheap ranged poke. Carian Greatsword sweeps a crowd. Loretta's Greatbow lets you start a fight from far away.

## Why these pieces

- **Moonveil** comes from the Magma Wyrm in Gael Tunnel, a Caelid dungeon you can reach early. The Guide tab has the route. It needs 23 Intelligence and 18 Dexterity, and its damage scales with Intelligence.
- **Glintstone Staff** is the Prisoner's own staff, so you start with a catalyst that can cast every spell here.
- **Land of Reeds set**: light, and it keeps a medium roll at low Endurance.

## Stats

Intelligence first: 31 covers Loretta's Greatbow (26), and both the katana and the spells keep improving with it. Then Vigor to 26 and Dexterity to Moonveil's 18. Keep raising Intelligence and Vigor as you go, and the build grows into a full Moonveil spellblade.
