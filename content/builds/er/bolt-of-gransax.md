Bolt of Gransax, the dragon's lightning spear, at level 150 with Faith for buffs and a Golden Greatshield. A Dexterity/Faith lightning build.

## How it plays

The Bolt of Gransax is a spear that deals lightning damage. Its skill, Ancient Lightning Spear, throws a huge bolt that reaches across an arena, hits hard, and can be charged for more. Open fights with it, then poke with the spear's quick thrusts. The Cleanrot Spear in the other slot brings Sacred Phalanx, for holy damage against enemies that resist lightning.

Before bosses, cast Golden Vow, Flame, Grant Me Strength and Blessing of the Erdtree (which heals you over time) from the seal.

## Why these pieces

- **Golden Greatshield** blocks whatever gets past the spear. With it equipped the build is a heavy roll, so take it off when a fight calls for dodging.
- **Radagon's Soreseal** adds 5 each to Vigor, Endurance, Strength and Dexterity, at the cost of taking more damage. **Ritual Sword Talisman** boosts your attack while your HP is full, which suits fighting at range.
- **Shard of Alexander** boosts the skill and **Carian Filigreed Crest** makes it cheaper.
- **Consort's Mask and the Leyndell Knight set**: solid protection for the weight.

## Stats

Dexterity to 49 (54 with the Soreseal) for the spear, Faith 38 for Blessing of the Erdtree, Strength 34 for the spear and shield. The rest goes into Vigor, Mind and Endurance.
