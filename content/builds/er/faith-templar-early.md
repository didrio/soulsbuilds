An early Faith knight from the Confessor: a Noble's Slender Sword with Golden Vow, a Brass Shield and two incantations. For your first 50 levels.

## How it plays

This is a safe, steady build for a first playthrough. Raise the Brass Shield with Barricade Shield active and most early enemies bounce off it. Then counter with the sword. Before a hard fight, use the sword's Golden Vow skill to boost your attack and defense. That leaves your FP free for the incantations: Blessing's Boon heals you over time, and Aspects of the Crucible: Tail gives you a sweep that covers your flanks.

If you'd rather have a ranged option, put Sacred Blade on the sword instead of Golden Vow.

## Why these pieces

- **Noble's Slender Sword** is light and quick, and it takes Ashes of War.
- **Brass Shield** with **Barricade Shield** makes blocking much cheaper on stamina.
- **Finger Seal** is the Confessor's own seal and casts both incantations.
- **Mausoleum Knight Armor, Greathelm and Knight pieces**: plenty of protection while keeping a medium roll.

## Stats

Faith to 27 for Aspects of the Crucible: Tail, Strength to 16 for the shield and the sword, and Endurance to 20 so blocking and rolling don't run you dry. The rest goes into Vigor.
