A Vagabond strength build for the first 50 levels: a Lordsworn's Greatsword with Golden Vow, a Claymore with Determination, and heavy Banished Knight armor.

## How it plays

You carry two greatswords and fight in heavy armor. Before a fight, use Golden Vow on the Lordsworn's Greatsword to boost your attack and defense, then switch to the Claymore and use Determination: your next attack hits much harder. Two-hand whichever sword you're using; two-handing adds half your Strength to your damage and stagger. Charged heavy attacks break most early enemies' stance.

## Why these pieces

- **Lordsworn's Greatsword** carries **Golden Vow** here. Greatswords take most Ashes of War, so it's easy to swap later.
- **Claymore** is a second greatsword with a different moveset. It carries **Determination** for big single hits.
- **Banished Knight set**: high poise, so enemies don't knock you out of your swings. It's a heavy roll at this level; carry one greatsword at a time if you want to roll faster.

## Stats

Endurance and Strength first (30 and 25): Endurance carries the armor and the greatswords, Strength makes them hit. Vigor next, to 26. Dexterity stays at 13, the Lordsworn's Greatsword's requirement.
