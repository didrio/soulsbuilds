A crystal sorcery mage from the Astrologer: Crystal Torrent melts bosses, Great Glintstone Shard opens fights, and a Carian sword and shield cover close range.

## How it plays

Keep your distance and keep casting. Great Glintstone Shard is your main ranged attack, Shattering Crystal handles enemies that get close, and Crystal Torrent is the boss melter: a stream you hold on the target while your FP lasts. Against magic-resistant enemies, switch to Rock Sling, which deals physical damage and staggers. Open boss fights with Scholar's Shield on the Carian Knight's Shield so you can block safely while you cast.

## Why these pieces

- **Crystal Staff** boosts crystal sorceries. It needs 48 Intelligence, the build's level-80 target.
- **Carian Knight's Sword and Shield** come from Liurnia. The sword is a backup for enemies you can't kite, and the shield blocks magic well.
- **Ritual Sword Talisman** boosts your attack at full HP, so it rewards fighting from range. **Godfrey Icon** boosts charged spells, **Magic Scorpion Charm** raises magic damage, and **Cerulean Amber Medallion +2** gives a bigger FP pool.
- **Astrologer set**: the class's own robes, light, with a fast roll.

## Stats

Intelligence to 48 first, for the staff and Crystal Torrent (47). Then Mind for FP and Vigor for safety. Endurance stays low: a mage doesn't need much.
