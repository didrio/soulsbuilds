The Tarnished Pack's Heavy Knight as an endgame tank: 80 Strength, a Gilded Greatshield and a Heavy Hefty Scimitar. Patch 1.17.

## How it plays

You block a lot and you hit hard after you block. Cast Cragblade on the scimitar before the boss reaches you, then raise the Gilded Greatshield. Learn which hit ends each of the boss's combos. Block that hit, answer with one guard counter, and lower the shield between attacks so your stamina comes back. Once the boss's stance breaks, take the critical hit. Against fast bosses, a plain light attack after the block is the safer punish.

Patch 1.17 gave the Gilded Greatshield more guard boost and less weight.

## Why these pieces

- **Gilded Greatshield**'s own skill is No Skill, so the left hand never takes over the scimitar's Cragblade.
- **Great-Jar's Arsenal** carries the heavy weapon, the greatshield and the full Steel set while keeping a medium roll.
- **Curved Sword Talisman** makes guard counters hit harder, **Greatshield Talisman** makes blocking cost less stamina, and **Bull-Goat's Talisman** helps the Steel set's poise absorb hits while you swing.
- **Greenburst Crystal Tear** gives faster stamina recovery and the **Opaline Hardtear** cuts all damage. Eat a **Boiled Crab** before bosses that hit with mostly physical damage.
- If a boss hits too hard, swap Bull-Goat's for Dragoncrest Greatshield, or for the Drake talisman that matches the damage type.

## Stats

60 Vigor, 40 Endurance for stamina and equip load, and 80 Strength. Everything else stays at the class's base apart from 14 Mind, enough for a few Cragblades.
