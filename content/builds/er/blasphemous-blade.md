The Blasphemous Blade with 60 Strength and 40 Faith: a fire greatsword whose skill heals you with every hit, the classic Strength/Faith build.

## How it plays

The Blasphemous Blade's skill, Taker's Flames, sends a wide wave of fire forward, and every enemy it hits restores some of your HP. That makes it a strong opener and a heal when you need one. Between skills, swing it as a normal greatsword, two-handed for more damage and stagger. Before bosses, cast Golden Vow and Flame, Grant Me Strength from the Erdtree Seal.

## Why these pieces

- **Blasphemous Blade** comes from Rykard's remembrance, traded at the Roundtable Hold.
- **Erdtree Seal** scales with Faith, which this build raises to 40.
- **Shard of Alexander** boosts Taker's Flames, **Godfrey Icon** boosts charged skills and spells, and the **Axe Talisman** boosts charged attacks. **Sacred Scorpion Charm** raises holy damage, for holy incantations you add later.
- **The Knight set** is our pick: the source only lists helms. It gets the build to 51 poise and keeps a medium roll.

## Stats

60 Strength and 40 Faith; the blade scales with both. 50 Vigor, 20 Mind for a few Taker's Flames per flask, 28 Endurance for the greatsword and armor. Dexterity 15 is the blade's requirement.
