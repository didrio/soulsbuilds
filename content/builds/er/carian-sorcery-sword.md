The Carian Sorcery Sword from Shadow of the Erdtree: a thrusting sword that casts sorceries too, at 72 Intelligence with Rellana's Twin Moons.

## How it plays

The Carian Sorcery Sword is a thrusting sword that also works as a staff. You don't need a catalyst in the other hand: one weapon both stabs and casts. Mix quick thrusts and Impaling Thrust up close with sorceries at range. Glintblade Trio and Greatblade Phalanx keep pressure on, and Rellana's Twin Moons is the big opener. Scholar's Shield blocks hits while you cast, and the Wolf Crest Shield's Carian Retaliation parries attacks, spells included.

## Why these pieces

- **Magic Scorpion Charm** and the **Magic-Shrouding Cracked Tear** raise magic damage. The **Opaline Hardtear** cuts damage taken.
- **Blade of Mercy** boosts your attack after a critical hit, which the shield's parries set up. **Two-Headed Turtle Talisman** speeds up stamina recovery.
- **Carian Knight Helm, Rellana's Armor and Greaves, Beast Champion Gauntlets** reach 51 poise at a medium roll.
- **Radahn's Great Rune** raises HP, FP and stamina.

## Stats

72 Intelligence, Rellana's Twin Moons' requirement, which also drives the sword's damage. 60 Vigor, 28 Mind for spells, 26 Endurance. Dexterity 19 and Strength 12 for the sword.
