Nagakiba with Chilling Mist and Cold affinity: a long katana that freezes everything it touches, with Dexterity and Intelligence. Level 125.

## How it plays

Nagakiba is the longest katana: it reaches farther than almost any other one-handed weapon. Chilling Mist sends a cloud of frost forward that builds up frostbite fast; a frostbite proc deals a burst of damage and makes the enemy take more damage for a while. Open with the mist, follow up with long katana swings while the enemy is frozen, and switch to the Black Bow to pull enemies one at a time.

## Why these pieces

- **Cold affinity** makes the katana scale with Intelligence and adds frost buildup to every hit.
- **Chilling Mist** fits the affinity.
- **Rotten Winged Sword Insignia** and **Millicent's Prosthesis** add damage as hits chain (getting both takes trading or a second playthrough). **Shard of Alexander** boosts Chilling Mist, and **Bull-Goat's Talisman** helps you swing through hits.
- **Zamor set**: frost-themed armor that keeps a medium roll.

## Stats

50 Dexterity and 40 Intelligence feed the Cold affinity's split scaling. 45 Vigor, 20 Endurance, Strength 18 for the katana. Faith and Arcane stay at the Samurai's base. Keep raising Dexterity and Intelligence past 125.
