The Tarnished Pack's Idus Knight, endgame: a Keen Idus Sword with Wing Stance, 77 Dexterity and two Faith buffs. Patch 1.17.

## How it plays

You keep the sword the class starts with. The Idus Sword is a light greatsword: longer reach than a straight sword, nearly as fast. Two-hand it, keep up the pressure with normal attacks, and use Wing Stance whenever the boss gives you a gap. If you hold the stance for a second before you attack, Rellana's Cameo gives that attack a big boost. The light follow-up is a quick three-hit flurry; the heavy follow-up is a leaping thrust for bigger openings.

Before a hard fight, cast Golden Vow and Flame, Grant Me Strength from the seal, drink the Physick, then go in.

## Why these pieces

- **Keen affinity** moves the sword's scaling onto Dexterity, so almost every level goes into one damage stat.
- **Wing Stance** comes from the Shadow of the Erdtree, at Castle Ensis. Until you have it, the sword's own Impaling Thrust is a cheap opener.
- **Shard of Alexander** and **Rellana's Cameo** boost the skill, **Rotten Winged Sword Insignia** rewards long strings of hits, and the **Two-Handed Sword Talisman** boosts every two-handed swing.
- **Thorny** and **Bloodsucking Cracked Tears** stack two more damage boosts. Bloodsucking drains your HP, so drink it once the boss's opening moves are out of the way.
- The **Rakshasa set** adds attack power and keeps a medium roll. The Silver Grooved set you start with is the safer, more on-theme choice.
- **Milady** (Keen) is a second light greatsword for when you want a different moveset.

## Stats

60 Vigor, then Dexterity all the way to 77. Endurance 25 keeps the roll medium, and Faith 25 casts Golden Vow. Strength, Intelligence and Arcane stay where the class starts them.
