Milady with Wing Stance at level 192: a Quality light greatsword from Castle Ensis, with 54 Strength and 60 Dexterity behind every flurry.

## How it plays

Milady is a light greatsword: long reach and fast, flowing combos. Wing Stance turns it into a stance weapon. Hold the stance, then a light attack fires three quick slashes, or a heavy attack does a leaping thrust. Hold the stance for a moment before you attack and Rellana's Cameo adds a big boost. A few rounds of stance attacks break the stance of large enemies such as giants.

Cast Golden Vow and Flame, Grant Me Strength before a boss, and use Radahn's Great Rune for more FP and stamina between flasks.

## Why these pieces

- **Milady** is in a chest at the top of a tower in Castle Ensis, and **Wing Stance** is in another Castle Ensis tower. You can get Milady without beating a DLC boss.
- **Quality** splits the sword's scaling evenly between Strength and Dexterity.
- **Rellana's Cameo** and **Carian Filigreed Crest** (cheaper skills) suit a build that uses its skill constantly. **Millicent's Prosthesis** rewards long combos, and **Dragoncrest Greatshield** makes up for the light armor.
- **Rellana's set** gets you to 51 poise.

## Stats

Dexterity to its 60 soft cap and Strength to 54, where Quality's scaling stops paying off. 60 Vigor, 25 Mind for the stance and buffs, 30 Endurance, 25 Faith for Golden Vow.
