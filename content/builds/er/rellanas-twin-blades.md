Rellana's Twin Blades from Shadow of the Erdtree: paired light greatswords that scale with all four damage stats, plus moon and fire projectiles.

## How it plays

The Twin Blades are a pair of light greatswords, one for magic and one for fire. They're fast enough for long combos and reach well for paired weapons. Their skill, Moon-and-Fire Stance, has two follow-ups: one sends magic projectiles at range, the other a fire burst up close that can stagger. Combo with normal attacks, enter the stance when there's an opening, and keep FP flasks handy: this build uses the skill a lot.

Cast Golden Vow and Flame, Grant Me Strength from the Golden Order Seal before bosses.

## Why these pieces

- **Rellana's Twin Blades** come from Rellana's remembrance, traded at the Roundtable Hold after Castle Ensis.
- **Golden Order Seal** scales with Intelligence and Faith, both of which this build already raises.
- **Rellana's Cameo** boosts the attack you make after holding a stance, and **Shard of Alexander** boosts the skill. **Millicent's Prosthesis** and **Rotten Winged Sword Insignia** add damage as hits chain (getting both takes trading or a second playthrough; Dragoncrest Greatshield is the easy swap).
- **Rellana's set** reaches 51 poise, enough not to be knocked out of your attacks by most enemies.

## Stats

An even spread: 32 Strength and 32 Dexterity, 23 Intelligence, 25 Faith. The blades scale with all four, and these numbers also open other weapons (Moonveil, the Blasphemous Blade). 50 Vigor, 30 Mind, 30 Endurance.
