Bloodfiend's Arm with Blood affinity: a DLC colossal weapon that builds up bleed fast, at 50 Arcane, in light Rakshasa armor.

## How it plays

Bloodfiend's Arm is a colossal weapon that builds up bleed quickly, and with Blood affinity Arcane drives both its damage and its bleed. Two-hand it and use charged heavy attacks. They break stances, and each bleed proc takes a chunk of the boss's health. The Uchigatana is only there for Seppuku: use it before a fight for its buff, then switch back to the Arm.

Golden Vow and Flame, Grant Me Strength from the Finger Seal round out the opener. The Rakshasa armor makes you take more damage, so play carefully.

## Why these pieces

- **Blood affinity** gives the Arm an Arcane-scaling bleed buildup.
- **Lord of Blood's Exultation** boosts your attack whenever bleed procs nearby, and the **White Mask** does the same.
- **Two-Handed Sword Talisman** boosts two-handed attacks, the **Axe Talisman** boosts charged attacks, and **Blade of Mercy** boosts your attack after a critical hit.
- **Rakshasa Armor, Gauntlets and Greaves** add attack power, at the cost of defense.

## Stats

50 Arcane for damage and bleed. 60 Vigor because the armor is thin, 36 Endurance to carry the colossal weapon at a medium roll, 30 Strength, 25 Faith for Golden Vow. Dexterity stays at 11: the Uchigatana only needs to cast Seppuku, not swing well.
