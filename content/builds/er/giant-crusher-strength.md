Giant-Crusher at 80 Strength: a colossal hammer that ends fights in a few charged hits, with Radahn's Great Rune and a Mimic Tear.

## How it plays

Giant-Crusher is the heaviest kind of hammer in the game: slow, huge damage, enormous stance damage. Two-hand it and use charged heavy attacks and jump attacks; most bosses lose their stance after a few. Your poise lets you trade hits instead of dodging everything. Summon the Mimic Tear for boss fights, and cast Flame, Grant Me Strength from the Finger Seal first.

## Why these pieces

- **Giant-Crusher** needs 60 Strength; at 80 it's near its best.
- **Great-Jar's Arsenal** and **Erdtree's Favor +2** carry the hammer and armor at a medium roll (Erdtree's Favor also adds HP and stamina).
- **Claw Talisman** boosts jump attacks and the **Axe Talisman** charged attacks: this build's two best moves.
- **Scaled Helm and Gauntlets, Raptor's Black Feathers** (more jump attack damage) and **Bull-Goat Greaves**: high poise for a medium roll.
- **Spiked Cracked Tear** boosts charged attacks further. The **Cerulean Hidden Tear** removes FP costs for a while.
- **Flame, Cleanse Me** cures poison and rot.

## Stats

80 Strength, 40 Endurance for the weight and stamina, 50 Vigor. Faith 15 for the two incantations and Dexterity 18 for Giant-Crusher's other options. Mind stays low: the build barely uses FP.
