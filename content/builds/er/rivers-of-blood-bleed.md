A Dexterity/Arcane bleed build that lives on Corpse Piler, with enough FP to use it all fight long. Checked against patch 1.17.

## How it plays

Rivers of Blood is your main attack, and its skill does most of the work: Corpse Piler sweeps a wide arc of cursed blood that builds up bleed on everything it touches, groups included. Most fights go the same way: close the gap, fire one or two Corpse Pilers, step out, repeat. Normal swings are for finishing off weakened enemies and for saving FP.

Before a boss, swap to the seal in your left hand and cast Flame, Grant Me Strength. Rivers of Blood deals physical and fire damage, so the buff boosts both.

## Why these pieces

- **Sacrificial Axe** in the left hand gives back a little FP for every kill, even if you never swing it. Long dungeon runs stop draining your flasks.
- **Dragon Communion Seal** scales with Arcane, so the build casts its buff without raising Faith past 15.
- **Lord of Blood's Exultation** and **Rotten Winged Sword Insignia** raise your damage once bleed procs and as hits chain together. **Shard of Alexander** boosts the skill, and **Carian Filigreed Crest** makes it cheaper to use.
- If you run out of stamina, swap the Crest for the **Green Turtle Talisman**.
- The armor is just a medium roll with some poise. Wear whatever looks right; the White Mask is the one piece that matters, since it boosts your attack when bleed procs nearby.

## Stats

Arcane comes first because it drives bleed buildup and the katana's damage. Dexterity is next, then 58 Vigor to survive late-game hits and 30 Mind for frequent skill use. Strength 16 is there for the Sacrificial Axe, Faith 15 for the buff.
