An Intelligence build around Ranni's Dark Moon Greatsword: a frost greatsword that fires moonlight waves, with heavy sorceries behind it. Patch 1.17.

## How it plays

The Dark Moon Greatsword is the build's melee weapon and a ranged weapon too. Its skill, Moonlight Greatsword, coats the blade in moonlight (more damage, frost buildup); while the coating lasts, charged heavy attacks fire a moonlight wave. Coat the blade at the start of a fight and use the waves to start fights from range.

The scepter covers the rest. Comet and Ranni's Dark Moon are big single hits for openings. Greatblade Phalanx keeps blades circling you while you swing, and Terra Magica boosts magic damage when you stand in its circle.

## Why these pieces

- **Carian Regal Scepter** boosts full moon sorceries, so Ranni's Dark Moon hits harder.
- **Shard of Alexander** boosts the skill, **Godfrey Icon** boosts charged spells and skills, and **Magic Scorpion Charm** raises magic damage. **Dragoncrest Greatshield Talisman** makes up for the light defense.
- **Carian Knight set**: about the heaviest armor that still keeps a medium roll at 20 Endurance, and it fits the build.

## Stats

80 Intelligence: the sword, the scepter and Ranni's Dark Moon (68) all scale with it. 55 Vigor, 30 Mind for the spells, and Strength 16 for the sword.
