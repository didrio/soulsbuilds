Paired Backhand Blades with Blood affinity: a fast Dexterity/Arcane bleed build from Shadow of the Erdtree, light enough to dodge anything.

## How it plays

Backhand Blades are paired, so you swing both at once. Their own skill, Blind Spot, is a quick sidestep that ends in a slash: a cheap way to dodge and hit at the same time, and it costs little enough FP to use all fight long. Every hit builds up bleed. Before a fight, use Seppuku from the Uchigatana to coat it in blood, then go back to the blades.

## Why these pieces

- **Blood affinity** adds Arcane-scaling bleed buildup to the blades.
- **Uchigatana** carries **Seppuku**; it's used two-handed, since the build keeps Strength at 10.
- **Lord of Blood's Exultation** and the **White Mask** boost your attack whenever bleed procs nearby. **Rotten Winged Sword Insignia** adds more with every hit in a string. **Retaliatory Crossed-Tree** boosts the attack right after a roll or backstep, and the **Two-Headed Turtle Talisman** speeds up stamina recovery.
- **Gravebird armor** is light, so you keep a fast roll with room to spare.
- **Finger Seal** casts Golden Vow and Flame, Grant Me Strength.

## Stats

55 Dexterity and 45 Arcane: Dexterity for the blades' damage, Arcane for bleed. 50 Vigor, 14 Mind for about ten Blind Spots per flask, 25 Faith for Golden Vow, and Strength at the blades' minimum of 10.
