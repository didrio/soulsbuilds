A Dexterity build around Bloodhound's Fang, the curved greatsword you can get from a Limgrave boss. Strong from the early game to the end; level 100 here.

## How it plays

Bloodhound's Fang is a curved greatsword that builds up bleed with every swing. Its skill, Bloodhound's Finesse, is a backflip slash that leaves you with a follow-up lunge: use it to hit and get out of range in one move, then lunge back in. With 51 poise, you can trade hits with most enemies without losing your attack.

## Why these pieces

- **Bloodhound's Fang** drops from the Bloodhound Knight in Forlorn Hound Evergaol, in Limgrave. The Guide tab has the route.
- **Bull-Goat's Talisman** brings the armor to 51 poise, the point where most normal enemies can't interrupt your attacks.
- **Shard of Alexander** boosts Bloodhound's Finesse; the **Claw Talisman** boosts jump attacks, the curved greatsword's best opener. **Dragoncrest Greatshield** cuts physical damage hard.
- **Raptor's Black Feathers** boosts jump attacks too; the Knight helm, gauntlets and greaves bring the poise up while keeping a medium roll.

## Stats

Dexterity is the damage stat, at 50 here. Strength 18 and Dexterity 17 are the weapon's requirements, Vigor 45 is for survival, and the rest goes into Endurance for stamina. Keep raising Dexterity and Vigor past level 100.
