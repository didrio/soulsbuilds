The Tarnished Pack's Idus Knight for your first hours: guard counters with the starting sword and shield, a holy Ash of War, and two cheap incantations.

## How it plays

The Silver Grooved Shield blocks all physical damage, so this build plays defensively. Block an enemy's last hit, answer with a guard counter, and take the critical hit once its stance breaks. Assassin's Crimson Dagger heals you on every critical hit, so each stance break also tops up your HP. Against skeletons and enemies out of reach, use Sacred Blade: it fires a holy projectile and adds holy damage to the sword for a while.

Keep Urgent Heal for when the flasks run out, and Flame Sling for enemies that shrug off holy damage.

## Why these pieces

- **Sacred Blade** comes from a Teardrop Scarab by the Third Church of Marika in Limgrave.
- **Assassin's Crimson Dagger** is the Black Knife Assassin's reward in Deathtouched Catacombs, and the same dungeon has a Deathroot. Trade the Deathroot to Gurranq at Bestial Sanctum for the **Clawmark Seal**.
- **Urgent Heal** and **Flame Sling** are sold by Brother Corhyn at Roundtable Hold. The Idus Knight's starting 11 Faith casts both.
- **Crimson Crystal Tear** heals in an emergency; the **Strength-knot Crystal Tear** gives +10 Strength for three minutes.
- The **Silver Grooved set** is the class's own armor and keeps a medium roll.

The Guide tab lists every stop in order.

## Stats

Vigor to 20 first: the class starts with only 10. Then Strength to 14 for the Clawmark Seal, and the rest into Dexterity for the sword. Upgrading the sword adds more damage early on than Dexterity does. After Stormveil, the Idus Knight Dex build picks up from here.
